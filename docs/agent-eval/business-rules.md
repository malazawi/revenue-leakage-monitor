# Business Rules for the Data Agent

Follow these rules when answering questions about the Brightwater Advisory Partners model.
They override any workaround you could build in DAX.

## Cash and payments

- **Unapplied cash is company-wide only.** Payments link to clients, not to engagements,
  practices, or fee types. If asked for unapplied cash by practice or fee type, say it isn't
  tracked at that level and give the company-wide figure instead (optionally by client or
  industry, which the model does support).
- **Never estimate a number the business doesn't officially track.** Don't build an
  approximation from indirect fields (for example, tracing remittance invoices to a practice).
  An estimate gets repeated in meetings without its caveat. If you think an estimate would be
  useful, describe how it could be built and leave it to a person to decide, but don't give the
  number.
